# North Star

## Vision
Stronghold Survivors is a space-extraction survival defense game where you build modular kill-mazes around yourself and fragile extractors, then move zone-to-zone as the planet pushes back. The maze is not decoration. It is your weapon.

## Core Fantasy
You crash-land on a hostile planet, carve out temporary strongholds, harvest extraction zones under siege, then relocate and rebuild. You are not a hero with a gun. You are a combat engineer surviving long enough to be picked up.

## Player Promise
- If the player can fit, enemies can fit.
- If a maze looks viable, it works.
- If an upgrade drops, it changes how you fight.

## Pillars
- Maze-first defense. Pathing is smart and reliable.
- Zone pressure. You must move to keep up with scaling threat.
- Readable chaos. Swarms are big, but decisions are clear.
- Visible power. Towers, upgrades, and FX must feel loud.
- Tension without cheapness. You die because of choices, not unreadable noise.

## Core Loop
1. Land with minimal gear.
2. Claim a zone and build a micro-base.
3. Funnel enemies through your maze and stabilize.
4. Extract until zone depletes or pressure forces a move.
5. Relocate, rebuild, escalate.
6. Final hold: choose a zone, build the fortress, survive the pickup.

## Experience Beats
- 0 to 5 min: fast setup, micro-fort, first relocation.
- 5 to 12 min: larger zones, meaningful maze choices, pressure spikes.
- 12+ min: final hold, fortress becomes alive, all systems firing.

## Combat Identity
- Player gun is for survival and relocation, not main DPS.
- Towers are the primary damage engine.
- Enemies always hunt the player through the maze.

## Visual North Star
- Space-industrial pixel art with high contrast silhouettes.
- Clear value separation between terrain, towers, enemies, and FX.
- Strong lighting cues on towers and projectiles.
- Resource zones read as alien energy fields, not simple circles.

## Animation Standard
- Player: 8-direction, 8 frames per direction.
- Enemies: 4 to 6 frames per direction for core types.
- Towers: 4 to 6 frames for fire and idle glow.
- FX: punchy, high contrast, short lifetimes.

## FX Standard
- Hits and kills feel explosive and readable.
- Projectiles leave trails and leave impact signatures.
- Color coding by damage type stays consistent.

## Non-Goals
- Perfect realism.
- Overly complex tech trees.
- Visual noise at the expense of clarity.
